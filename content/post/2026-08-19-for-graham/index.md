OK, let’s see if we can make this work, albeit a few days into the trip.

### Day 1  Sunday

It’s hard work packing the van for 6 weeks, for 2 people. Go on, gentle reader and check just how many pairs of underwear you have - I’ve just packed 3 weeks worth.

First stop is a pub stop in rural Herefordshire (or is it Worcestershire, the borders all seem to blur in these border counties). Anyway the King’s Head in Docklow not only welcomes us to sleep in their car park overnight but also has a beer festival. The one thing it failed to mention is that it’s not much of a car park! We arrive, much in need of a beer, to find the car park full and an overflow field being used. Make that an overflow ploughed field! We park, nose down like a Stuka on a bombing run, obviously not am an incline we’d be inclined to sleep at and wander into the pub. It's a token system. 10 tokens = 5  pints = £25 . We persuade them to sell us 2 tokens for a fiver. There were a handful of beers on offer, Liz had Alsopps Bitter and I think my beer was Grinbull. We decide not to stay despite the beers being good.

Plan B was the nearby Stockton Cross.A phone call and sorted but they close their kitchen at 4. So another half at the Kings Had beer festival (same again for Liz. Same brewery ., different beer for me). But the smash burgers they lubricants were excellent.

Stockton Cris was 5 miles up the road, Wye Valley HPA and Butty Bach were well kept, but just as important the car park was as level as a master mason’s bubble. A couple of points and a good nights sleep .
